Drag the target around the ring, or click the arena and press ← → (Shift: 15°). The shaded wedge is what the camera can see.

The body stays still and the head turns. The shaded wedge is what the camera sees (its width is assumed), the dashed lines are the head's turn limit, and the marker is where the camera last reported the face. It cannot be dragged: move in front of the robot instead.

The circuit, from eye to turn

LC10a
Small-object detectors. Each strip is one eye; each neuron sits at the spot it watches (the dog's left on the left).
AOTU019
Inhibits the opposite side's steering neuron. Driven by frontal targets.
AOTU025
Excites the same side's steering neuron. Driven by lateral targets.
DNa02
Steering neurons, shown as change from their resting rate. The difference, left minus right, sets the turn.
