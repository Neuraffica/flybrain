"Truth" is the IMU's fused yaw. With the cue on, that same yaw is also given to the models on every sample (about 5 per second) as a stand-in for a visual landmark, so cue-on error mostly measures lag. Angular velocity is derived from the same yaw (the OpenCat firmware prints no raw gyro rate) and goes to all three models on every tick. Blindfolded, the models get angular velocity only.
